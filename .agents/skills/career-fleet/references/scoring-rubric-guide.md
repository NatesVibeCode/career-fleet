# 4-Tier Career Scoring Rubrics & Quote Verification Guide

Every assessment must be grounded in exact verbatim quotes extracted from captured source documents (job postings, engineering blogs, founder interviews).

### Tier 1: High Fit (Score 0.80 - 1.00)
- Meets all 6 core signals.
- Confirmed hiring catalyst directly aligned with candidate wedge.
- Verifiable engineering depth (stateful systems, durable execution, core data rails).
- Workplace arrangement matches the policy selected in the user's profile.

### Tier 2: Strong Fit (Score 0.60 - 0.79)
- All configured required-stack entries are evidenced, with strong problem-wedge alignment.
- Healthy leadership signals and workplace evidence that matches the selected profile policy.
- Minor unknowns in operational distribution or team topology.

### Tier 3: Marginal / Hold (Score 0.40 - 0.59)
- Acceptable technology but unclear product defensibility or commoditized niche, or a configured required-stack entry is missing.
- Unknowns around office mandate, workplace preference, or headcount trajectory.

### Tier 4: Disqualified (Score 0.00)
- Violates any hard dealbreaker (mandatory in-office, headcount > limit, shallow wrapper, or pure quota role).
