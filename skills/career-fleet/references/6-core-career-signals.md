# The 6 Core Career Signals Framework

Every high-signal candidate-opportunity match rests on resolving these 6 signals:

### 1. Architectural & Domain Wedge
The specific high-leverage problem the candidate solves. Not a generic resume summary, but a concrete capability statement (e.g. 0-to-1 go-to-market architecture, distributed runtime engineering, high-throughput event streaming).

### 2. Required Tech Stack & Environment
The core technologies and infrastructure the target employer must operate in production (e.g. PostgreSQL, Kafka, Kubernetes, Go, Python). Avoid companies running legacy silos or incompatible architectures.

### 3. Hard Disqualifiers / Anti-Profile
Non-negotiable dealbreakers that disqualify a company on sight:
- Headcount bloat (> 80–100 employees where politics replace building)
- Mandatory non-local in-person office mandates (e.g. 4–5 days in SF/NYC)
- Shallow AI wrappers (prompt-over-Playwright with no proprietary moat)
- Pure quota boiler rooms (cold-call hamster wheels with zero product influence)

### 4. The Hiring Catalyst
The specific operational failure, milestone, or architectural transition that creates urgent leadership mandate and budget (e.g. scaling off v1 prototype, moving beyond founder-led sales).

### 5. Target Leader Persona
The founder or engineering leadership profile: low-ego technical builders who welcome direct technical and commercial diagnosis over corporate PR.

### 6. Anchor Exemplars
2–3 companies representing the gold standard of product depth and culture.
